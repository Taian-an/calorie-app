PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Problem

"Every time I eat at the cafeteria, I have no idea how many calories I just consumed. Apps like MyFitnessPal require me to manually search every dish — I wanted something simpler: just take a photo."
1
Manual entry is slow & inaccurate
Searching local dish names in English databases returns wrong or missing results.
2
Barcode scanners don't help
Cooked cafeteria meals and homemade food have no barcode to scan.
3
Generic goals ignore your body
A flat "2,000 kcal/day" doesn't account for age, height, weight or activity level.
4
No combined free solution
No free app offers AI photo recognition + personalised TDEE + macro planning together.

USE CASE 01

AI Food Logging — Step by Step

Actor: Student at cafeteria  ·  Goal: Get nutrition without typing
1
Open Camera Tab
Navigate to 📷 Camera. App requests camera permission on first launch.
2
Frame Food & Tap Shutter
Point phone at meal → tap circular shutter button. Photo captured locally.
3
AI Recognition
Photo sent to nateraw/food via Hugging Face API → returns label + confidence, e.g. "fried_rice — 94%".
4
Nutrition Lookup
Food label queried against USDA FoodData Central → Energy, Protein, Carbs, Fat per 100g.
5
Result Card Shown
Result card slides up with food name, confidence badge, 4 macro tiles. Total: ~2–3 seconds.
Demo / Screenshot
USE CASE 02

Personalised Diet Planning — Step by Step

Actor: Gym-goer (muscle-gain)  ·  Goal: Get personalised macro targets
1
Open Calculator Tab
Navigate to 🧮 Calculator tab → select gender (Male / Female).
2
Enter Body Data
Type age, height (cm), weight (kg). All three fields required before calculation.
3
Select Activity Level
Choose from 5 levels: Sedentary ×1.2 → Lightly Active ×1.375 → Moderate ×1.55 → Very Active ×1.725 → Athlete ×1.9.
4
View TDEE Result
Mifflin-St Jeor: BMR = 88.36 + 13.4×kg + 4.8×cm − 5.7×age. TDEE = BMR × factor. Shown in ring gauge.
5
Choose Goal Mode
Tap Muscle Gain → Protein 30% · Carbs 50% · Fat 20% — gram amounts & kcal shown with progress bars.
